Great landing pages are not accidents. They are engineered systems where every element earns its place.

## Start with one promise

Your hero section should answer three questions in under five seconds:

- What is this?
- Who is it for?
- Why should I care?

<Callout type="info">
The headline is not a tagline. It is a contract with the reader.
</Callout>

## Structure for scanning

Most visitors scan before they read. Use:

1. A clear headline and subhead
2. Social proof above the fold
3. Benefit-driven sections, not feature lists
4. A single primary CTA repeated at natural decision points

## Microcopy matters

Button text like "Submit" wastes an opportunity. Try:

- "Get the free guide"
- "Start my 14-day trial"
- "See pricing for my team"

## Test what you assume

The best copywriters I know run small experiments constantly. Change one variable, measure, learn, repeat.

## Key takeaway

Conversion is a design problem *and* a language problem. When structure and voice align, results follow.
