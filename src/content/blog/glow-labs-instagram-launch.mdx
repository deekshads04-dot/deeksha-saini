When Glow Labs came to me, they had a great product and zero social presence. Six months later, we hit 50K followers and meaningful revenue.

## Phase 1: Foundation

We defined:

- Brand voice pillars
- Visual mood board
- Content pillars (education, lifestyle, social proof)

## Phase 2: Consistency

We posted 5x per week for 90 days without missing a beat. Consistency compounds.

## Phase 3: Reels-first growth

Reels accounted for 78% of new followers. We optimized for saves and shares.

## Results

- 50K followers in 6 months
- 8.2% average reel engagement
- ₹2.4M first-month launch revenue

The playbook is repeatable. The execution has to be relentless.
