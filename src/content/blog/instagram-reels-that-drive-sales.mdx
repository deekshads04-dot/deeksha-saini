Viral views feel great. Revenue feels better. Here is how I bridge the gap.

## The hook is everything

You have roughly 1.5 seconds. Open with:

- A bold claim
- A relatable problem
- Visual pattern interrupt

## Sell the transformation

Do not sell the product in frame one. Sell the outcome:

> "Clear skin in 14 days" beats "Our new serum has retinol."

## Use native CTAs

Link in bio is fine. Comments and DMs convert faster when you prompt them in the reel itself.

<Callout type="success">
Pin a comment with your offer link. Repeat the CTA verbally at the end.
</Callout>

## Batch and repurpose

One shoot day should fuel:

- 4–6 reels
- 2 carousel posts
- Stories with polls and Q&A

## Measure what matters

Track saves and shares, not just likes. Those signals predict purchase intent.
