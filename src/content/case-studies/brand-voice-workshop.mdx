A B2B client was publishing content that sounded like five different companies. We fixed that in one workshop.

## The challenge

- Inconsistent tone across blog, ads, and sales decks
- No documented voice guidelines
- Founder vision not translating to the team

## The process

### Day 1: Discovery

We interviewed stakeholders and audited 50+ content pieces.

### Day 2: Definition

We built a voice matrix with four dimensions:

| Dimension | Before | After |
|-----------|--------|-------|
| Formality | Mixed | Confident, approachable |
| Humor | Random | Light, sparing |
| Technical depth | Too high | Accessible expert |
| CTA style | Passive | Direct, helpful |

<Callout type="warning">
Without examples, voice guidelines become wallpaper. We wrote 20 before/after samples.
</Callout>

## Outcomes

- 40% faster content approval cycles
- Unified messaging across 6 channels
- Template library the non-writing team could use

## Lesson

Brand voice is not a PDF. It is a shared language your whole team can speak.
